# 3D twin body

`body.glb`: "X Bot" character from Adobe Mixamo (as shipped in the three.js examples, `examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb`),
reduced for the app with glTF-Transform: animations and the joint-ring mesh removed, textures dropped, geometry quantized.
Mixamo characters may be used royalty-free inside projects; they must not be redistributed as stand-alone assets.

The skeleton, organs, vessels and knee implant drawn inside the body are generated in code (`twin-src/main.js`)
from the rig's bone positions. Illustrative only: not anatomically exact.
