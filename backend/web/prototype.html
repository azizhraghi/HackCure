Start clean

Resets the demo patient (care plan, vitals history, photos) and the demo clock: day 3 after discharge, 15:30, inside a permitted dressing-change window.

1

Documents become the care plan

D2 COMPILERD3 GROUNDING

Sarah uploads her discharge summary. Developer 2's compiler extracts 20+ clinical fields and runs its deterministic allergy/interaction graph. The shared backend then checks every medicine, dose and date against the text and lists anything to verify. Nothing is active until Sarah confirms.

2

What a normal day looks like

D1 TRAJECTORY · TWIN

From the plan (the "D2 JSON"), Developer 1 estimates an uncomplicated recovery for this operation and this person, split into days, which the app's home screen shows. The Patient Twin continues Sarah's own heart-rate and SpO₂ curve a few hours ahead, or abstains and says why.

3

Something changes at home

D3 SAFETY · INVESTIGATION

Sarah's check-in says she is very tired; her wearable shows heart rate and temperature creeping up. Urgent rules run first (no AI). Nothing is urgent, so the investigation agent gathers evidence, asks Sarah one question and asks for a wound photo, but only because a dressing change is permitted now.

4

The care team closes the loop

D3 ALERTS · FOLLOW-UP

The clinic sees the evidence report and the alert. Acknowledging, contacting Sarah, recording the action, verifying with new evidence and resolving are separate steps in the clinic view. Ahmed (family) gets only what Sarah allowed.
